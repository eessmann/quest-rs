# Optimization implementation and references

This map connects the [optimizer interfaces](../book/src/optimization.md) to their research references, implementation and focused regressions. The papers motivate the approaches; they do not establish correctness or speedup for this implementation. Focused regressions are linked below. The [final validation and timing results](2026-09-28-optimization-roadmap-results.md) are recorded separately; this map makes no performance claim. For changed caller contracts, see the [migration guide](2026-09-28-optimization-roadmap-migration.md) and runnable [ideal](../../crates/quest/examples/optimize.rs) and [worker](../../crates/quest/examples/optimize_workers.rs) examples.

## Exact foundation and publication boundary

The maintained MIT [mathcore fork](../../crates/vendor/mathcore/UPSTREAM.md) supplies bounded exact affine algebra through [quest-symbolic](../../crates/quest-symbolic/src/lib.rs); [Angle](../../crates/quest-circuit/src/model.rs) retains quest-owned owner, source, binding, and finite-conversion obligations. Independent [affine-pi certification](../../crates/quest-math/src/approx.rs) encloses the original `r+s*pi` target with directed arithmetic and full `4*pi` rotation phase. Binding produces binary64 execution radians **and** an immutable exact target sidecar; opaque binary64 leaves and OpenQASM numerical evaluation remain distinct. Parent-side [worker clients](../../crates/quest-optimizer-client/src/lib.rs) reconstruct and verify certificates before a candidate can be published. The [foundation tests](../../crates/quest-circuit/tests/symbolic_contract.rs) and [mathcore provenance](../../crates/vendor/mathcore/UPSTREAM.md) delimit this trust boundary; the [migration guide](2026-09-28-optimization-roadmap-migration.md) explains fallible APIs and retained cancelled-parameter obligations.

## Boundaries shared by the directions

- [Optimizer contracts](../../crates/quest-circuit/src/optimizer_contracts.rs) distinguish `LocalCertified` proof history from `GlobalCertified` whole-program evidence. The reported cumulative error is the exact-rational sum of admitted **local** allowances along the selected history against the original input; it is a whole-program bound only with `GlobalCertified`. Native floating execution error and numerical fusion rounding are outside mathematical synthesis certificates. Required-global mode rejects unsupported composition and skips uncertified fusion.
- [Native cost](../../crates/quest-circuit/src/optimizer_contracts.rs) uses actual allocated-register deployment and caller-declared reuse, defaulting to one. MPI communication that cannot be established stays unknown; a changed ordered communication component is unscorable, not zero cost. Cost estimates predict selection, not native timing.
- [Structured orchestration](../../crates/quest-circuit/src/structured_pipeline.rs) includes bounded flow analysis, static exact cleanup and terminal fusion, but **skips worker beam search** and records `WorkerSearch` in `skipped_stages()`. Unknown branch frequencies, dynamic/opaque effects and calls are not assigned invented costs. [Structured terminal fusion](../../crates/quest-circuit/src/structured_terminal.rs) leaves pure zero-wire windows unchanged and does not replace user-gate bodies; the [circuit operation model](../../crates/quest-circuit/src/model.rs) still retains explicit global phase, including phase made observable by coherent control.
- The [measurement fixtures](fixtures/optimization-roadmap/README.md) preserve failed, capped and timed-out rows and separate compiler proposal timings from native execution. Their completion manifests, hardware modes, comparisons and actual timing outcomes belong in the [results document](2026-09-28-optimization-roadmap-results.md), not in a gate-count speedup claim.
